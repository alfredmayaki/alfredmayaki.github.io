Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 5 — Wednesday, February 11, 2026

🎿 Day 5 Overview

Day 5 brings the men's super-G on the legendary Stelvio course, Nordic combined individual, biathlon women's 15km individual, freestyle skiing aerials qualification, luge women's final, and women's 1000m speed skating. A bumper schedule with medals across six disciplines.

⭐ Day 5 Highlights

  • Men's Super-G medal event on the Bormio Stelvio — a brutally technical course
  • Nordic Combined Individual — ski jumping & cross-country combined
  • Biathlon Women's 15km Individual in Anterselva
  • Luge Women's Singles Final (Runs 3 & 4) at Cesana
  • Speed Skating Women's 1000m medal event at Baselga di Piné
  • Freestyle Skiing Aerials Qualification at Livigno
⛷️
