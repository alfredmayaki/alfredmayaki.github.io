About Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki

Labour Economist · Public Policy Researcher · Human Resource Business Partner

Biography

Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki is British-Nigerian labour economist and public policy researcher with extensive experience in human resource management and evidence-based policy development. His work spans applied labour market analysis, workforce planning, and the evaluation of interventions that promote inclusion and well‑being at work. Alfred combines quantitative research methods with pragmatic HR practice, working with academic partners and public sector stakeholders to translate evidence into actionable policy.

Alfred holds postgraduate qualifications in Economics and has contributed to research projects hosted by The Open University and the Royal Economic Society. He is active in community initiatives and student groups, and frequently collaborates with cross-disciplinary teams on surveys, web-scraping tools for research, and accessible dissemination of academic outputs.

Outside of research, Alfred supports organisations as a Human Resource Business Partner, advising on talent strategies, employee relations, and organisational development. He is committed to accessible communication and multilingual outreach — this site is available in English and multiple other languages.
