Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 15 — Saturday, February 21, 2026

🎿 Day 15 Overview

The penultimate day of competition brings a massive twelve medal events as the Milano-Cortina Games approach their climax. The alpine team event creates electric head-to-head racing in Cortina, the biathlon women's 12.5km mass start delivers a frenetic pack-start shootout at Anterselva, the cross-country women's 30km mass start is the sport's ultimate endurance test, bobsled crowns its two-woman champion and begins the four-man event, freestyle aerials finals light up the Livigno night sky, short track's men's 5000m relay produces a chaotic team final, speed skating's mass start events reward tactical cunning, and ice hockey reaches its men's and women's semifinals.

⭐ Day 15 Highlights

  • Alpine Team Event — head-to-head parallel racing in Cortina
  • Biathlon Women's 12.5km Mass Start — pack racing at altitude
  • Cross-Country Women's 30km Mass Start Free — the marathon on skis
  • Bobsled Two-Woman Final (Runs 3 & 4) at Cesana
  • Bobsled Four-Man — Runs 1 & 2 at Cesana
  • Freestyle Skiing Men's & Women's Aerials Finals at Livigno
  • Short Track Men's 5000m Relay Final — team gold in Milan
  • Speed Skating Men's & Women's Mass Start — tactical gold
  • Ice Hockey — Men's & Women's Semifinals
⛷️

Alpine Skiing

Mixed Team Parallel
Alpine Skiing — Knockout Format
10:00
📍 Cortina d'Ampezzo — Olympia delle Tofane
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇦🇹Austria — deep across all disciplines, traditional team event powerhouse with Schwarz, Feller, Liensberger, Huber. 🇨🇭Switzerland — Odermatt, Meillard, Gut-Behrami, Holdener — Swiss versatility is unmatched. 🇩🇪Germany — 2022 Olympic team event champions, tactical masters of the parallel format. 🇳🇴Norway — Kristoffersen leads a strong Norwegian squad in head-to-head racing.
📊 Records — Mixed Team Parallel
Olympic Champions: Austria (PyeongChang 2018, inaugural), Germany (Beijing 2022)
The team event pits nations head-to-head in parallel slalom — two men and two women per round, rapid-fire knockout format. The crowd atmosphere is uniquely electric, and upsets are common.
🎯

Biathlon

Women's 12.5km Mass Start
Biathlon
14:30
📍 Anterselva — Südtirol Arena
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇫🇷Justine Braisaz-Bouchet (FRA) — 2024 overall World Cup champion, explosive racer with sharp shooting. 🇫🇷Lou Jeanmonnot (FRA) — breakout French star, fearless in pack racing. 🇮🇹Dorothea Wierer (ITA) — Italian icon, two-time overall champion, home-crowd heroine at Anterselva. 🇸🇪Elvira Öberg (SWE) — rising Swedish star, consistent World Cup form.
📊 Records — Women's 12.5km Mass Start
Olympic Champion: Justine Braisaz-Bouchet (FRA) — 40:18.0 (Beijing 2022)
The mass start is biathlon's most spectacular format — 30 athletes start together and race side-by-side to four shooting stages. Clean shooting is everything; one miss can cost a medal. The atmosphere at Anterselva for Wierer's farewell could be extraordinary.
🎿

Cross-Country Skiing

Women's 30km Mass Start Free
Cross-Country Skiing
10:00
📍 Tesero — Lago di Tesero
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇳🇴Therese Johaug (NOR) — if competing; 2022 Olympic 30km champion, Norwegian distance queen, the greatest female cross-country skier of her generation. 🇸🇪Frida Karlsson (SWE) — 2022 Olympic 30km silver, Swedish endurance, dramatic finisher. 🇫🇮Kerttu Niskanen (FIN) — Finnish distance strength, 2022 Olympic 10km classic champion. 🇷🇺Natalia Nepryaeva (AIN) — if eligible; Russian distance dominance, 2022 skiathlon silver.
📊 Records — Women's 30km Mass Start
Olympic Champion: Therese Johaug (NOR) — 1:24:54.0 (Beijing 2022)
The women's 30km is the ultimate endurance test in women's cross-country skiing — over 90 minutes of relentless racing through the Trentino forests. It traditionally closes the women's programme and is one of the most emotional events of the Games.

Bobsled

Two-Woman — Runs 3 & 4 (Final)
Bobsled
14:00
📍 Cesana — Pariol Sliding Centre
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇩🇪Laura Nolte (GER) — defending Olympic champion, German piloting excellence. 🇺🇸Elana Meyers Taylor (USA) — four-time Olympic medallist, American legend seeking gold. 🇨🇦Christine de Bruin (CAN) — Canadian monobob medallist, versatile pilot.
📊 Records — Two-Woman Bobsled
Olympic Champion: Laura Nolte & Deborah Levi (GER) — 3:56.89 (Beijing 2022)
The final two runs decide the champion — margins are measured in hundredths of a second. Meyers Taylor has been second three times at the Olympics; Milano-Cortina could be her final chance at the top step.
Four-Man — Runs 1 & 2
Bobsled
19:30
📍 Cesana — Pariol Sliding Centre
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇩🇪Francesco Friedrich (GER) — 2018 & 2022 Olympic four-man champion, seeking a three-peat in bobsled's blue-ribbon event. 🇨🇦Justin Kripps (CAN) — Canadian four-man consistency, Olympic two-man co-champion. 🇬🇧Brad Hall (GBR) — British four-man pilot, 2022 Olympic fourth place, agonisingly close to a medal. 🇦🇹Benjamin Maier (AUT) — Austrian sliding pedigree, powerful push team.
📊 Records — Four-Man Bobsled
Olympic Champion: Francesco Friedrich (GER) — combined 3:54.30 (Beijing 2022)
The four-man is bobsled's original and most prestigious event — a pilot and three pushers hurtle down Cesana's track at 150+ km/h. Friedrich's dominance is unprecedented: he has won the last two Olympic four-man golds.
🎿

Freestyle Skiing

Men's & Women's Aerials — Finals
Freestyle Skiing
19:00
📍 Livigno — Carosello 3000
🏅 MEDAL EVENT (×2)
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇨🇳Qi Guangpu (CHN) — 2022 Olympic men's aerials champion, Chinese aerial mastery with the most difficult tricks in the world. 🇨🇳Xu Mengtao (CHN) — 2022 Olympic women's aerials champion, Chinese legend competing at possibly her final Games. 🇺🇦Oleksandr Abramenko (UKR) — 2018 Olympic champion, Ukrainian courage on the world stage. 🇦🇺Danielle Scott (AUS) — Australian aerial veteran, World Championship medallist. 🇧🇾Hanna Huskova (BLR) — 2018 Olympic women's champion, Belarusian technical excellence.
📊 Records — Aerials
Men's Champion: Qi Guangpu (CHN) — 129.00 pts (Beijing 2022)
Women's Champion: Xu Mengtao (CHN) — 108.61 pts (Beijing 2022)
Aerials athletes launch off kickers at 70+ km/h, performing triple somersaults with multiple twists 15 metres above the snow. Under the Livigno floodlights, the spectacle is breathtaking — one landing decides everything.
⛸️

Short Track Speed Skating

Men's 5000m Relay — Final
Short Track Speed Skating
18:00
📍 Milan — PalaItalia Santa Giulia
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇨🇦Canada — 2022 Olympic 5000m relay champions, Dubois & Hamelin lead a deep squad. 🇰🇷South Korea — traditional relay powerhouse, Hwang Dae-heon anchors. 🇮🇹Italy — home-crowd energy at PalaItalia could be worth tenths of a second. 🇨🇳China — Wu Dajing leads a formidable Chinese relay squad.
📊 Records — Men's 5000m Relay
Olympic Champions: Canada — 6:41.257 (Beijing 2022)
The men's 5000m relay is 45 laps of coordinated team skating — four athletes take turns, pushing each other into the race at high speed. Falls, disqualifications, and photo finishes are routine. It is the loudest event in the Winter Olympics.
⛸️

Speed Skating

Men's & Women's Mass Start
Speed Skating
15:00 / 16:00
📍 Milan — Barentsburg Arena
🏅 MEDAL EVENT (×2)
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇧🇪Bart Swings (BEL) — 2022 Olympic men's mass start champion, Belgian tactical genius and sprint finisher. 🇳🇱Irene Schouten (NED) — 2022 Olympic women's mass start champion (plus 3000m & 5000m gold), Dutch dominance. 🇰🇷Chung Jae-won (KOR) — 2022 Olympic men's mass start silver, Korean tactical awareness. 🇨🇦Ivanie Blondin (CAN) — 2022 Olympic women's mass start silver, Canadian pack-racing savvy.
📊 Records — Mass Start
Men's Champion: Bart Swings (BEL) — 7:47.97 (Beijing 2022)
Women's Champion: Irene Schouten (NED) — 8:14.73 (Beijing 2022)
The mass start is speed skating's most unpredictable event — all skaters start together for 16 laps, with intermediate sprint points and a final sprint for gold. Positioning, drafting, and timing the final attack are everything.
