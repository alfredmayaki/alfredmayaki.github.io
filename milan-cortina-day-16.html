XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 16 — Sunday, February 22, 2026

🎿 Day 16 Overview — The Final Day

The last day of the Milano-Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics brings the Games to a spectacular close. The cross-country men's 50km mass start free — the marathon on skis — is the traditional final endurance event, a gruelling race through the Trentino forests at Tesero. Bobsled crowns its four-man champion in the blue-ribbon event of sliding sports at Cesana. Ice hockey reaches its climax with the men's and women's gold medal games at PalaItalia Santa Giulia. And as evening falls, the world gathers at San Siro for the Closing Ceremony — a celebration of 16 days of competition, 109 gold medals, and the unity of sport.

⭐ Day 16 Highlights

  • Cross-Country Men's 50km Mass Start Free — the ultimate endurance test at Tesero
  • Bobsled Four-Man Final (Runs 3 & 4) — bobsled's blue-ribbon event at Cesana
  • Ice Hockey Women's Gold Medal Game
  • Ice Hockey Men's Gold Medal Game — the final event before the Closing Ceremony
  • Closing Ceremony at Stadio San Siro — farewell to Milano-Cortina 2026

⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇳🇴Johannes Høsflot Klæbo (NOR) — dominant Norwegian sprinter-turned-distance star, 2022 Olympic relay gold, seeking the 50km crown. 🇷🇺Alexander Bolshunov (AIN) — if eligible; 2022 Olympic 30km skiathlon and 50km champion, Russian distance machine. 🇳🇴Simen Hegstad Krüger (NOR) — 2018 Olympic skiathlon champion, Norwegian endurance pedigree. 🇫🇮Iivo Niskanen (FIN) — 2022 Olympic 15km classic champion, Finnish distance grit. 🇫🇷Hugo Lapalus (FRA) — rising French distance talent, strong in mass start formats.
📊 Records — Men's 50km Mass Start
Olympic Champion: Alexander Bolshunov (ROC) — 1:11:32.7 (Beijing 2022)
The men's 50km is the longest and most demanding event in cross-country skiing — over 70 minutes of relentless racing through the forests surrounding Lago di Tesero. It has traditionally been the final cross-country event of the Games, lending it enormous symbolic weight. The athlete who wins the 50km is considered the ultimate endurance champion of the Winter Olympics.

Bobsled

Four-Man — Runs 3 & 4 (Final)
Bobsled
14:00
📍 Cesana — Pariol Sliding Centre
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇩🇪Francesco Friedrich (GER) — seeking a historic three-peat in bobsled's most prestigious event, 2018 & 2022 Olympic four-man champion. 🇨🇦Justin Kripps (CAN) — Canadian four-man consistency, two-man co-champion from 2018. 🇬🇧Brad Hall (GBR) — British pilot who finished agonisingly fourth in Beijing, seeking a breakthrough medal. 🇦🇹Benjamin Maier (AUT) — Austrian sliding pedigree with a powerful push team. 🇱🇻Oskars Ķibermanis (LAT) — Latvian veteran, World Cup podium regular.
📊 Records — Four-Man Bobsled
Olympic Champion: Francesco Friedrich (GER) — combined 3:54.30 (Beijing 2022)
The four-man bobsled is the original and most iconic event of the sliding sports — a pilot and three pushers hurtle down Cesana's Pariol track at 150+ km/h. Friedrich's dominance is unprecedented: he is the two-time defending champion and seeks to become the first man in history to win three consecutive Olympic four-man golds. The final two runs decide everything; margins are measured in hundredths of a second.

The Canada–USA rivalry defines women's Olympic hockey — the two nations have contested every gold medal game since 1998. Marie-Philip Poulin has scored the game-winning goal in three consecutive Olympic finals for Canada.

🎆 Closing Ceremony

Stadio San Siro, Milan — 20:00 CET

As the sun sets over the Alps and the final medals are awarded, the world gathers at the legendary Stadio San Siro for the Closing Ceremony of the XXV Olympic Winter Games. Sixteen days of competition across the mountains and arenas of northern Italy come to their conclusion — 109 events, thousands of athletes, and millions of moments that defined Milano-Cortina 2026. The Olympic flame is extinguished, the flag passes to the next host, and the athletes of the world celebrate together one last time beneath the Milan sky.

📍 Stadio Giuseppe Meazza (San Siro), Milan — Capacity: 80,018
