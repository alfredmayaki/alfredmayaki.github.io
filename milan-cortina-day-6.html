Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 6 — Thursday, February 12, 2026

🎿 Day 6 Overview

Day 6 features the women's giant slalom on the iconic Olympia delle Tofane in Cortina, biathlon men's 20km individual at Anterselva, cross-country skiing men's 15km classic, freestyle skiing aerials finals, short track speed skating's first medal events, and luge men's opening runs. A packed programme spanning six venues across northern Italy.

⭐ Day 6 Highlights

  • Women's Giant Slalom medal event on Cortina's Olympia delle Tofane
  • Biathlon Men's 20km Individual — the classic discipline at Anterselva altitude
  • Cross-Country Skiing Men's 15km Individual Classic at Tesero
  • Freestyle Skiing Aerials Finals (Men's & Women's) at Livigno
  • Short Track Speed Skating — Mixed Team Relay medal event in Milan
  • Luge Men's Singles — Runs 1 & 2 at Cesana

Biathlon

Men's 20km Individual
Biathlon
14:30
📍 Anterselva / Antholz — Südtirol Arena
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇳🇴Johannes Thingnes Bø (NOR) — most decorated biathlete of the era, 2022 Olympic 20km gold. 🇫🇷Quentin Fillon Maillet (FRA) — Beijing 2022 five-medal haul, 20km silver. 🇸🇪Sebastian Samuelsson (SWE) — 2018 Olympic relay gold, elite shooting accuracy. 🇳🇴Sturla Holm Lægreid (NOR) — World Championship gold, consistent World Cup performer.
📊 Records — Men's 20km Individual
Olympic Champion: Johannes Thingnes Bø (NOR) — 48:36.5 (Beijing 2022)
Each missed shot adds 1 minute; the 20km individual is biathlon's original Olympic event (since 1960). Anterselva's altitude (1,600 m) tests cardiovascular endurance.
