1. Introduction

Alfred Anate Bodurin Mayaki ("I", "me", "my") operates this website and the MSc Survey application ("the Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit the website and use the survey application.

Primary Purpose: The Application is designed to collect research data for an MSc dissertation project on neurodiversity in recruitment, supervised by Dr. Nicola J. Bowman at The Open University.

Research Context: By participating in this survey, you are contributing to academic research. Your data will be used to understand experiences of neurodivergent individuals in recruitment processes.

2. Information I Collect

2.1 Google OAuth Information

When you sign in using Google, I collect:

  • ✅Google Account ID (unique identifier)
  • ❌Email Address
  • ❌Full Name
  • ❌Profile Picture
  • ❌Email Verification Status

I do NOT request or store your Google password. Google handles all authentication securely. I only receive a verification token confirming your identity.

2.2 Survey Responses

When you submit the survey, I collect your responses to 7 questions about:

  • What attracts you to an organization
  • How you perceive employer inclusivity
  • Your recruitment experiences
  • Neurodivergence disclosure decisions
  • Adjustments during recruitment
  • Your best performance in recruitment
  • Any additional comments

2.3 Metadata

I automatically collect:

  • Submission Timestamp (date and time)
  • Browser & Device Info (for technical support)
  • Submission Status (successful/failed)
What I DON'T Collect: I do NOT collect your IP address, location data, browsing history, or any personal information beyond what you explicitly provide through Google authentication and survey responses.

3. How I Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Uses (Research)

  • Analyze recruitment experiences of neurodivergent individuals
  • Identify patterns in employer inclusivity signals
  • Contribute to academic knowledge in organizational psychology
  • Generate findings for dissertation and potential publications

3.2 Duplicate Prevention

I use your Google Account ID to ensure each person completes the survey only once. This prevents spam and helps maintain data quality for research purposes.

3.3 Technical Support

I may use your information to troubleshoot technical issues, respond to support requests, and improve the survey application.

3.4 What I DON'T Do

  • ❌ I do NOT sell your data to third parties
  • ❌ I do NOT use your data for marketing
  • ❌ I do NOT share your data without permission (except as required by law)
  • ❌ I do NOT use your data for profiling or automated decision-making

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Your survey responses are stored in two secure locations:

  • Google Sheets - Your response text is stored in a private Google Sheet (only accessible to the researcher)
  • Google Firestore - Submission tracking data (Account ID, timestamp, status) is stored for duplicate prevention

4.2 Security Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption - All data in transit is encrypted
  • Encryption at Rest - Google servers encrypt data at rest
  • Access Control - Only I (the author of this study) can access your data
  • OAuth 2.0 - Industry-standard authentication protocol
  • No Passwords - I never store or see your Google password
Security Commitment: I take data security seriously. All data is protected using Google Cloud's enterprise-grade security infrastructure.

5. Data Retention

5.1 How Long I Keep Your Data

Data Type Retention Period
Survey Responses 3-5 years (for research & compliance)
Submission Tracking (Firestore) 1-2 years (for duplicate prevention)
Audit Logs 1 year

5.2 After Retention Period

Once the retention period expires, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized. If you request deletion earlier, I will comply (see Section 6 - Your Rights).

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the European Union, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

6.1 Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of all personal data I hold about you. I will provide this in a clear, portable format within 30 days.

6.2 Right to Correction

If your personal information is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to request correction. Note: You cannot edit survey responses, but you can provide corrections about your account information.

6.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You have the right to request deletion of your data, subject to certain exceptions:

  • ✅ Can request deletion before data analysis begins
  • ⚠️ After analysis begins, deletion may affect research validity
  • ❌ Cannot request erasure of anonymized data

6.4 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to request your data in a portable, machine-readable format (e.g., CSV, JSON) to transfer to another service.

6.5 Right to Restrict Processing

You can request that I limit how I use your data while I verify its accuracy or assess your rights.

6.6 Right to Object

You can object to my processing your data for research purposes. However, this may prevent your participation in the survey.

6.7 Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe I have violated your rights, you can lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (e.g., ICO in the UK).

How to Exercise Your Rights: Contact Alfred Mayaki at the email address in Section 9 (Contact Information) with your request. I will respond within 30 days.
