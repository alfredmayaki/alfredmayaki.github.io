Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

📅 Day 3 — Sunday, February 9, 2026

🎿 Day 3 Overview

Sunday delivers the first blockbuster medal day of Milano-Cortina 2026. Seven gold medals are up for grabs across six sports — headlined by the Freestyle Skiing Moguls finals, the Women's Giant Slalom in Cortina, the Men's Slopestyle Snowboard final, the Biathlon Mixed Relay, and the electrifying Short Track Mixed Team Relay. The Figure Skating Team Event continues with Pairs and Women's short programmes, while Luge Men's Singles begins its first two runs.

⭐ Day 3 Highlights

  • Seven gold medals on offer — the busiest medal day so far 🥇🥇🥇🥇🥇🥇🥇
  • Men's & Women's Moguls Finals — Walter Wallberg and Jakara Anthony defend their crowns 🎿
  • Women's Giant Slalom — Federica Brignone bids for home gold on the Tofana 🇮🇹
  • Men's Slopestyle Snowboard Final — Su Yiming vs Max Parrot redux 🏂
  • Biathlon Mixed Relay — France seek back-to-back Olympic golds 🎯
  • Short Track Mixed Team Relay — Italy chase gold in front of the Milanese crowd ⛸️
  • Cross-Country Skiathlon — Johannes Høsflot Klæbo headlines the Tesero trails 🏔️
