# Privacy Policy Update - Search Tracking

## 📝 Add This Section to Your Privacy Policy

When you enable site-wide search tracking, add this section to your privacy policy:

---

## Search Analytics

### What We Collect

When you use the search feature on our website, we collect the following **anonymous** information:

- **Search queries**: The text you enter in the search box
- **Timestamp**: When the search was performed
- **Browser information**: Browser type and language preference
- **Referrer**: The website or page you came from (if any)

### What We DO NOT Collect

We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information:

- ❌ IP addresses
- ❌ Names or email addresses
- ❌ Account information
- ❌ Location data (unless you explicitly share it)
- ❌ Browsing history beyond searches

### How We Use This Data

We use search analytics to:

- Understand what topics interest our visitors
- Improve our content and services
- Identify trends and common questions
- Display aggregated search statistics on our "Search Tag Cloud" page

### Data Storage

- **Personal searches** are stored locally in your browser (localStorage)
- **Aggregated searches** are stored in Firebase Realtime Database
- Data is stored indefinitely unless you clear your browser data
- You can request deletion of your search history at any time

### Third-Party Services

We use **Google Firebase** (owned by Google LLC) to store and analyze search data. Firebase complies with:

- ✅ GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- ✅ CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- ✅ Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Your Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access**: View your personal search history in the tag cloud
2. **Delete**: Clear your local search data at any time
3. **Opt-out**: Disable JavaScript or use private browsing mode
4. **Export**: Download your search data as JSON

### Data Sharing

We **do not sell, rent, or share** your search data with third parties. The only sharing that occurs is:

- Aggregated, anonymized search statistics displayed publicly on the "Search Tag Cloud" page
- Firebase's processing of data (as our service provider)

### Children's Privacy

Our search feature does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided search data, please contact us to have it removed.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when changes were made.

### Contact Us

If you have questions about our search data practices:

- Email: [Your email]
- Website: [Your website]
- GitHub: https://github.com/alfredmayaki

## 🎯 Best Practices

### 1. Be Transparent
- Link to privacy policy from homepage
- Mention search tracking clearly
- Show the tag cloud publicly

### 2. Give Users Control
- Clear data button on tag cloud page
- Export data option
- Easy opt-out instructions

### 3. Minimize Data Collection
- Only collect search queries (no personal data)
- Don't track IP addresses
- Avoid collecting unnecessary metadata

### 4. Respect Privacy
- Anonymous by design
- No user profiling
- No targeted advertising
- No data selling
