Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 9 — Sunday, February 15, 2026

🎿 Day 9 Overview

Sunday delivers one of the busiest medal days so far. The men's super-G on the legendary Stelvio in Bormio opens the speed events for the men, biathlon pursuit races crown two champions in Anterselva, the men's moguls final lights up Livigno, the luge men's singles concludes at Cesana, ski jumping men fly off the normal hill at Predazzo, the short track women's 500m decides the fastest woman on ice, and the women's 5000m in speed skating tests pure endurance. Nine medal events make this a golden Sunday across the Italian Alps.

⭐ Day 9 Highlights

  • Men's Super-G — speed kings tackle the Stelvio in Bormio
  • Biathlon Men's 12.5km & Women's 10km Pursuits — chase-format drama at Anterselva
  • Cross-Country Men's Skiathlon 15km+15km — classic-to-freestyle endurance test
  • Freestyle Skiing Men's Moguls Final — gold medal run at Livigno
  • Luge Men's Singles Final (Runs 3 & 4) at Cesana
  • Short Track Women's 500m Final — explosive sprint for gold in Milan
  • Ski Jumping Men's Individual Normal Hill — medal event at Predazzo
  • Speed Skating Women's 5000m — endurance classic at Barentsburg Arena
  • Mixed Doubles Curling — Gold & Bronze Medal Games in Cortina
