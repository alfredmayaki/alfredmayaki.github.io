Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 2 — Sunday, February 8, 2026

🎿 Day 2 Overview

Day 2 delivers the first moguls medals, women's giant slalom action, the exciting biathlon mixed relay, short track heats, and the cross-country skiathlon. Medal events across multiple disciplines ensure a packed schedule in Milan, Cortina, and Anterselva.

⭐ Day 2 Highlights

  • Freestyle Skiing Men's & Women's Moguls Finals — first freestyle medals
  • Women's Giant Slalom Run 1 on the Tofana course
  • Biathlon Mixed Relay medal event in Anterselva
  • Short Track Speed Skating heats at Mediolanum Forum
  • Cross-Country Skiathlon opens distance racing in Tesero
