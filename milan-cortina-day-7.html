Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 7 — Friday, February 13, 2026

🎿 Day 7 Overview

Day 7 delivers one of the marquee events of any Winter Olympics — the men's downhill on the legendary Stelvio course in Bormio. The programme also features the biathlon mixed relay, ski jumping men's individual normal hill, short track speed skating 1500m heats, luge men's final runs, figure skating team event, and snowboard cross qualification. An action-packed Friday across the Italian Alps.

⭐ Day 7 Highlights

  • Men's Downhill — the blue riband event on Bormio's fearsome Stelvio
  • Biathlon Mixed Relay — fast-paced team drama at Anterselva
  • Ski Jumping Men's Individual Normal Hill medal event at Predazzo
  • Short Track Speed Skating — Men's 1500m heats & Women's 500m heats in Milan
  • Luge Men's Singles Final (Runs 3 & 4) at Cesana
  • Figure Skating Team Event continues at the Milano Forum

⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇨🇭Marco Odermatt (SUI) — overall World Cup king, Bormio winner, seeking downhill gold. 🇦🇹Vincent Kriechmayr (AUT) — 2021 World champion in downhill & super-G, Stelvio specialist. 🇨🇭Beat Feuz (SUI) — 2022 Olympic downhill champion (if competing), Stelvio maestro. 🇳🇴Aleksander Aamodt Kilde (NOR) — 2020 downhill globe winner, explosive speed, if fit. 🇫🇷Cyprien Sarrazin (FRA) — breakout 2024 Bormio downhill winner, fearless descender.
📊 Records — Men's Downhill
Olympic Champion: Beat Feuz (SUI) — 1:42.69 (Beijing 2022)
Stelvio Record: Dominik Paris (ITA) — 1:54.63 (Bormio 2019 WC)
The Stelvio is one of the most treacherous courses on the World Cup circuit — steep, icy, and technically demanding with speeds exceeding 140 km/h.
