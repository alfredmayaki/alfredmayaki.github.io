Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 4 — Tuesday, February 10, 2026

🎿 Day 4 Overview

Day 4 features the marquee women's downhill, women's ski jumping on the normal hill, luge women's runs, biathlon men's individual, and the first short track speed skating finals. Multiple medal events make this one of the biggest days of the Games.

⭐ Day 4 Highlights

  • Women's Downhill on home snow at Cortina — Sofia Goggia's golden shot
  • Women's Ski Jumping Normal Hill medal event at Predazzo
  • Luge Women's Singles Runs 1 & 2 at Cesana
  • Biathlon Men's 20km Individual in Anterselva
  • Short Track 1000m & 500m Finals at Mediolanum Forum
⛷️

Biathlon

Men's 20km Individual
Biathlon

📍 Anterselva / Antholz — Südtirol Arena
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
