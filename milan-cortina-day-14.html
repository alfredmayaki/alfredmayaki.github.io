Milano-Cortina 2026

XXV Olympic Winter Games

Day 14 — Friday, February 20, 2026

🎿 Day 14 Overview

Friday delivers one of the most anticipated days of the entire Games with eleven medal events. The women's slalom produces two breathtaking runs at Cortina, figure skating's women's free skate crowns the individual champion in Milan, the biathlon men's 4×7.5km relay brings national glory at Anterselva, the cross-country men's 50km mass start — the marathon of the snow — unfolds at Tesero, speed skating men's 1000m tests sprint power, bobsled two-woman begins its medal quest at Cesana, short track's women's 3000m relay delivers team drama, and freestyle skiing aerials qualification begins at Livigno. Curling gold medal matches and ice hockey quarterfinals raise the stakes.

⭐ Day 14 Highlights

  • Women's Slalom — two-run technical duel at Cortina's Olympia delle Tofane
  • Figure Skating Women's Free Skate — crowning the Olympic champion
  • Biathlon Men's 4×7.5km Relay — national pride at Anterselva
  • Cross-Country Men's 50km Mass Start Free — the ultimate endurance test
  • Speed Skating Men's 1000m — sprint power at Barentsburg Arena
  • Bobsled Two-Woman — Runs 1 & 2 at Cesana
  • Short Track Women's 3000m Relay Final — team gold in Milan
  • Freestyle Skiing Aerials Qualification at Livigno
  • Curling — Gold Medal Matches at Cortina
  • Ice Hockey — Men's Quarterfinals

Alpine Skiing

Women's Slalom
Alpine Skiing — Run 1 & Run 2
10:00 / 13:30
📍 Cortina d'Ampezzo — Olympia delle Tofane
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇺🇸Mikaela Shiffrin (USA) — most World Cup wins in history, three-time Olympic medallist, the greatest slalom skier ever with 60+ World Cup slalom victories. 🇸🇰Petra Vlhová (SVK) — 2022 Olympic slalom champion, Slovak national hero, fierce gate-attacking style. 🇩🇪Lena Dürr (GER) — German slalom specialist, consistent World Cup podium finisher, aggressive second runs. 🇸🇪Anna Swenn-Larsson (SWE) — Swedish precision, World Championship medallist. 🇨🇭Wendy Holdener (SUI) — Swiss technical excellence, multiple combined and slalom medals.
📊 Records — Women's Slalom
Olympic Champion: Petra Vlhová (SVK) — combined 1:44.98 (Beijing 2022)
Vlhová's victory at Beijing denied Shiffrin a record-extending medal; Cortina's Tofane course is steep and icy — perfect for technical mastery. Shiffrin seeks Olympic slalom redemption after her DNF at Beijing.

Figure Skating

Women's Singles — Free Skate
Figure Skating
17:00
📍 Milan — Milano Forum
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇯🇵Kaori Sakamoto (JPN) — back-to-back World Champion (2023, 2024), 2022 Olympic bronze, Japanese power and artistry, the woman to beat. 🇺🇸Isabeau Levito (USA) — 2024 World silver, American rising star with quad capability and balletic presentation. 🇰🇷Kim Ye-lim (KOR) — South Korean elegance, consistent under championship pressure. 🇧🇪Loena Hendrickx (BEL) — Belgian trailblazer, 2023 World bronze, fierce competitor.
📊 Records — Women's Singles
Olympic Champion: Anna Shcherbakova (ROC) — 255.95 total (Beijing 2022)
The women's free skate is 4 minutes of artistry, athleticism, and nerve — the greatest show on ice. Sakamoto's combination of triple-triple jumps and mesmerising choreography makes her the overwhelming favourite.

Biathlon

Men's 4×7.5km Relay
Biathlon
14:30
📍 Anterselva — Südtirol Arena
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇳🇴Norway — Bø, Lægreid, Christiansen, Dale — four-deep squad, Olympic relay dynasty. 🇫🇷France — Fillon Maillet, Jacquelin, Fabien Claude, Émilien Claude — 2022 Olympic relay bronze, rising French depth. 🇩🇪Germany — Doll, Nawrath, Kühn, Lesser — German shooting precision, always medal contenders. 🇸🇪Sweden — Samuelsson anchors a strong Swedish quartet with World Cup relay pedigree.
📊 Records — Men's 4×7.5km Relay
Olympic Champions: Norway — 1:19:50.2 (Beijing 2022)
The men's relay is biathlon's ultimate team event — four athletes each ski 7.5km with two shooting stages. Norway have dominated this event, winning 8 of the last 10 Olympic relay golds. Anterselva's altitude adds an extra layer of suffering.

Cross-Country Skiing

Men's 50km Mass Start Free
Cross-Country Skiing
10:00
📍 Tesero — Lago di Tesero
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇷🇺Alexander Bolshunov (AIN) — if eligible; 2022 Olympic 30km/50km champion, Russian distance machine. 🇳🇴Hans Christer Holund (NOR) — 2019 World 50km champion, Norwegian power over the longest distance. 🇳🇴Simen Hegstad Krüger (NOR) — 2018 Olympic skiathlon gold, Norwegian distance versatility. 🇫🇮Iivo Niskanen (FIN) — 2022 Olympic 15km classic gold, Finnish endurance and tactical intelligence.
📊 Records — Men's 50km Mass Start
Olympic Champion: Alexander Bolshunov (ROC) — 1:11:32.7 (Beijing 2022, run as 30km)
The 50km mass start is cross-country skiing's marathon — over two hours of relentless racing through the Trentino forests. It is traditionally the final cross-country event and one of the most gruelling tests in all of sport.

Speed Skating

Men's 1000m
Speed Skating
15:00
📍 Milan — Barentsburg Arena
🏅 MEDAL EVENT
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇳🇱Thomas Krol (NED) — 2022 Olympic 1000m champion, Dutch technical mastery on the 1000m distance. 🇰🇷Kim Jun-ho (KOR) — emerging Korean sprint talent, World Cup podium regular. 🇨🇦Laurent Dubreuil (CAN) — 2022 Olympic 500m silver, Canadian explosive speed across sprint distances. 🇳🇱Kjeld Nuis (NED) — 2018 Olympic 1000m & 1500m double champion, Dutch veteran seeking another medal.

Bobsled

Two-Woman — Runs 1 & 2
Bobsled
19:30
📍 Cesana — Pariol Sliding Centre
⭐ Favourites & Contenders 🇩🇪Laura Nolte (GER) — 2022 Olympic two-woman champion, German piloting precision. 🇺🇸Elana Meyers Taylor (USA) — most decorated female bobsled athlete in Olympic history with 4 medals, American power. 🇨🇦Christine de Bruin (CAN) — Canadian monobob bronze at Beijing, versatile pilot. 🇬🇧Mica McNeill (GBR) — British bobsled pioneer, improving World Cup results.

The two-woman event features a pilot and a brakewoman reaching speeds over 130 km/h through Cesana's 19 curves. Germany have dominated women's bobsled, winning 3 of the last 4 Olympic gold medals.
